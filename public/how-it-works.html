Final Countdown Timer

How the Final Countdown Timer works

The Final Countdown Timer is a free online countdown timer that plays The Final Countdown while the clock runs. The music is wired to the clock: it starts when you press Start, pauses when you pause, and stops the instant you reset. When the clock reaches zero you get a short fanfare.

There is nothing to install, no account to create and no ads. It works on phones, tablets and desktops. The site keeps a public count of visits and shares and uses Google Analytics for usage statistics; the privacy page lists exactly what is collected.

How to use the timer

  1. Type a duration into the box and press Enter or hit Start. It understands most ways of writing a time: 90 for ninety seconds, 5:00 for five minutes, 1:02:03 for just over an hour, and shorthand like 5m or 1h30m.
  2. Or skip the typing and tap one of the presets underneath.
  3. Start becomes Pause while the clock runs, then Resume. Pausing holds both the time and the music exactly where they are.
  4. Reset stops everything and puts the clock back to your chosen duration.

The last ten seconds turn yellow and pulse, so you get a warning without watching the screen. The remaining time is also shown in the browser tab title, so you can keep an eye on a timer running in a background tab.

Presets

The six preset buttons cover the durations people reach for most often:

  • 30 seconds and 1 minute: plank holds, sprint intervals, speed rounds in a game, brushing your teeth.
  • 3 minutes: steeping tea, a boxing round, a lightning talk.
  • 5 and 10 minutes: classroom activities, meeting segments, a warm-up, a short break.
  • 25 minutes: one Pomodoro focus session.

Three ways to play the music

YouTube

The default. It plays the track through YouTube's embedded player in privacy-enhanced mode, and the player stays visible while it plays. Paste any other YouTube URL to swap in a different song; a t= timestamp in the URL is honored, so you can start a video part-way through.

Synth

A version of the riff generated live in your browser with the Web Audio API, with bassline, drums and slapback echo. Nothing is streamed, so it keeps working when YouTube is blocked or unavailable, which is common on school and office networks. It also starts instantly.

Your own file

Pick any audio file from your device and it becomes the timer's soundtrack. The file plays locally and is never uploaded.

Share a timer

The Share this timer button gives you a link with the duration built in as a ?t= parameter: ?t=25m for a Pomodoro, ?t=3m for a boxing round. Whoever opens the link lands on the same timer, set and ready to start, so you can drop a ready-made countdown into a group chat, a lesson plan or a workout post.

On phones the button opens your usual share sheet. On desktop it copies the link to your clipboard. The count under the button shows how many times timers have been shared.
